Business Requirements
AUTHORIAL AGENT / VERSION 0.1 / 24 NOVEMBER 2026
A graph-native authorial agent for constructing and maintaining the author’s intellectual work.



Purpose and scope
The author remains the intellectual authority. The system constructs symbolic intellectual content before
prose, reuses the canonical loop, and maintains traceability from intent to expression and from requirements
to implementation and evidence.
Baseline status: proposed for review. The agreed architectural direction is distinguished from detailed
candidate requirements and design choices. No implementation or verification completion is asserted.



Reading the identifiers
BR-xx identifies a requirement category; BR-xx.yy identifies a subrequirement. DS-xx.yy identifies its initial
design realization. Mappings are many-to-many in the target graph; matching numbers are a convenient
initial allocation, not a restriction.



Architectural boundaries
Subject ontology: subject knowledge. Writing ontology: intellectual acts, composition and expression.
Authorial ontology: particular voice and exemplars. Operational ontology: canonical loops, evidence,
authority and closure. These are connected graph regions, not necessarily separate stores.
Success means the author can request a chapter and inspect its intent, knowledge, reasoning, composition,
prose, voice evidence and closure status. The agent must not silently substitute its own intellectual positions.

BR-01 Authorial ownership and authority
BR-01.01 | DS-01.01
Preserve the human as author; distinguish author-established positions, external evidence and agent
proposals.
Verification: An agent-originated claim cannot be represented as author-endorsed without recorded
authority.

BR-01.02 | DS-01.02
Respect delegated scope and seek human authorization for changes outside it.
Verification: An unapproved change to an authorial position is blocked or staged for review.



BR-02 Intent and input evidence
BR-02.01 | DS-02.01
Represent intent explicitly at work, chapter, section, paragraph and act scales as canonical-loop input
evidence.
Verification: Every construction loop resolves a versioned intent and relevant input evidence.

BR-02.02 | DS-02.02
Assess fulfilment of intent separately from conformance.
Verification: A conforming paragraph that misses its intent fails validation.

BR-03 Writing Domain Ontology
BR-03.01 | DS-03.01
Represent writing as persistent graph objects independent of subject and prose.
Verification: A work can be retrieved with its acts, knowledge, composition and expressions separately.

BR-03.02 | DS-03.02
Distinguish classes, attributes, roles, states and relationships.
Verification: A proposition used as premise and conclusion retains one identity.



BR-04 Knowledge and research
BR-04.01 | DS-04.01
Retrieve author knowledge and relevant external sources before constructing content; identify gaps.
Verification: A chapter plan identifies unsupported claims and records sources or open questions.

BR-04.02 | DS-04.02
Preserve proposition identity across epistemic state changes and track content revisions.
Verification: Refuting a proposition preserves its identity and history; a changed proposition links to its
predecessor.

BR-05 Intellectual construction and reasoning
BR-05.01 | DS-05.01
Construct intellectual acts before generating prose; support atomic and composite acts.
Verification: A chapter can be inspected as acts and dependencies without reading prose.

BR-05.02 | DS-05.02
Represent argumentation and intellectual dependencies.
Verification: A conclusion can be traced to premises, inference, assumptions, evidence and objections.



BR-06 Composition and literary structure
BR-06.01 | DS-06.01
Maintain intellectual and literary structures separately with many-to-many mappings.
Verification: Reordering paragraphs does not silently alter the underlying argument.

BR-06.02 | DS-06.02
Support reusable acts and multiple manuscript versions.
Verification: Two works may reference an act without duplicating its identity.

BR-07 Authorial voice and exemplars
BR-07.01 | DS-07.01
Represent linguistic, rhetorical, epistemic and conceptual voice rules.
Verification: Prompt construction can resolve applicable rules across all four layers.

BR-07.02 | DS-07.02
Use authentic positive, negative and corrective exemplars.
Verification: A voice rule can be traced to an exact source passage or author correction.



BR-08 Expression and prompt construction
BR-08.01 | DS-08.01
Generate prose as a realization of encoded intellectual structure, not as the authoritative knowledge source.
Verification: Alternative expressions can be generated without changing the proposition or act.

BR-08.02 | DS-08.02
Construct prompts from resolved symbolic inputs and preserve execution provenance.
Verification: A generated passage can be traced to the prompt, model and input versions.

BR-09 Checking, validation and closure
BR-09.01 | DS-09.01
Use the canonical loop for research, composition, expression, editing and learning.
Verification: Each operation has input evidence, output, checking, validation and closure evidence or an
explicit unresolved state.

BR-09.02 | DS-09.02
Separate criteria, instruments and findings; use Pangram as a signal, not proof.
Verification: A detector flag alone cannot establish authorship or justify automatic rejection.



BR-10 Editing and deficiency resolution
BR-10.01 | DS-10.01
Represent deficiencies as traceable discrepancies and distinguish findings from confirmed deficiencies.
Verification: A missing premise and a stylistic defect route to different appropriate operations.

BR-10.02 | DS-10.02
Allow writing to reveal research and reasoning gaps.
Verification: A discovered unsupported claim triggers research rather than cosmetic rewriting.

BR-11 Authorial learning
BR-11.01 | DS-11.01
Learn from author corrections without automatically generalizing local preferences.
Verification: A single correction is not promoted to a universal rule without justification.

BR-11.02 | DS-11.02
Separate subject learning from authorial learning.
Verification: A new subject fact does not silently modify a voice rule.



BR-12 Change propagation and integrity
BR-12.01 | DS-12.01
Track dependencies and reassess affected closures when relevant inputs change.
Verification: A refuted premise reopens dependent reasoning where justification fails; punctuation alone
does not reopen research.

BR-12.02 | DS-12.02
Preserve history and prevent silent overwrites.
Verification: Previous manuscript and evidence versions remain reconstructable.

BR-13 Platform integration and traceability
BR-13.01 | DS-13.01
Bind requirements, design, implementation and verification evidence bidirectionally through persistent
identities.
Verification: Every approved requirement resolves to design; implemented design resolves to code and
evidence, with missing links visible.

BR-13.02 | DS-13.02
Generate requirements and design documents from the authoritative graph and govern changes through the
same canonical architecture.
Verification: A changed requirement marks affected design and code links for reassessment; regenerated
documents show the new baseline.

Open decisions and integration gates
   Reconcile candidate classes and operations against the current platform catalog and canonical loop
    interfaces; establish actual enforcement points.
   Confirm authority matrix for authorial endorsement, rule generalization, criterion changes, ontology
    extensions and closure.
   Finalize intellectual-act taxonomy, epistemic states, composition cardinalities and revision semantics.
   Select model and Pangram integration, source permissions, retention, security, cost and latency targets.
   Define code registration granularity, deployment checks, telemetry coverage and independent
    verification protocol.
   Establish evaluation corpus of authentic writing and author-reviewed outputs; calibrate fidelity and
    intent-fulfilment criteria.



End-to-end acceptance scenario
Request a chapter distinguishing checking from validation. Resolve author intent and source knowledge;
construct and inspect the distinction and argument; select voice rules and exemplars; generate alternative
prose; check fidelity and validate intent; record closure evidence and authority. Change a premise and verify
selective reopening. Change a requirement and verify impact through design, code and evidence. Missing
links must remain visible, not be treated as successful verification.
