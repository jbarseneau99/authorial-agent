Design Specification
AUTHORIAL AGENT / VERSION 0.1 / 24 NOVEMBER 2026
A graph-native authorial agent for constructing and maintaining the author’s intellectual work.



Purpose and scope
The author remains the intellectual authority. The system constructs symbolic intellectual content before
prose, reuses the canonical loop, and maintains traceability from intent to expression and from requirements
to implementation and evidence.
Baseline status: proposed for review. The agreed architectural direction is distinguished from detailed
candidate requirements and design choices. No implementation or verification completion is asserted.



Reading the identifiers
BR-xx identifies a requirement category; BR-xx.yy identifies a subrequirement. DS-xx.yy identifies its initial
design realization. Mappings are many-to-many in the target graph; matching numbers are a convenient
initial allocation, not a restriction.



Architectural boundaries
Subject ontology: subject knowledge. Writing ontology: intellectual acts, composition and expression.
Authorial ontology: particular voice and exemplars. Operational ontology: canonical loops, evidence,
authority and closure. These are connected graph regions, not necessarily separate stores.
Candidate design contract: each object has a stable identity, version, declared type, attributes, typed
references, allowed operations, constraints and authority policy. Reuse existing platform classes where
possible. Do not declare new classes or capability extensions without the applicable authorization.

DS-01 Authorial ownership and authority
DS-01.01 | BR-01.01
Attach origin, endorsement and authority references to propositions and acts; route endorsement through
the existing approval mechanism.
Verification: An agent-originated claim cannot be represented as author-endorsed without recorded
authority.
Requirement: Preserve the human as author; distinguish author-established positions, external evidence and
agent proposals.

DS-01.02 | BR-01.02
Evaluate operation scope against delegated authority; bind decisions to the proposed revision and record
actor and evidence.
Verification: An unapproved change to an authorial position is blocked or staged for review.
Requirement: Respect delegated scope and seek human authorization for changes outside it.



DS-02 Intent and input evidence
DS-02.01 | BR-02.01
Reference existing Intent or input-evidence objects; connect parent and contributing intents with typed
edges.
Verification: Every construction loop resolves a versioned intent and relevant input evidence.
Requirement: Represent intent explicitly at work, chapter, section, paragraph and act scales as canonical-
loop input evidence.

DS-02.02 | BR-02.02
Resolve intent-derived criteria independently from structural checks; record both outcomes.
Verification: A conforming paragraph that misses its intent fails validation.
Requirement: Assess fulfilment of intent separately from conformance.

DS-03 Writing Domain Ontology
DS-03.01 | BR-03.01
Declare candidate Work, Composition, IntellectualAct and Expression types only after catalog reconciliation;
use typed references and versioned identities.
Verification: A work can be retrieved with its acts, knowledge, composition and expressions separately.
Requirement: Represent writing as persistent graph objects independent of subject and prose.

DS-03.02 | BR-03.02
Represent contextual roles on edges; enforce declared types and cardinalities at construction.
Verification: A proposition used as premise and conclusion retains one identity.
Requirement: Distinguish classes, attributes, roles, states and relationships.



DS-04 Knowledge and research
DS-04.01 | BR-04.01
Research loop resolves graph references, creates findings for gaps, and records source provenance and
evidence relationships.
Verification: A chapter plan identifies unsupported claims and records sources or open questions.
Requirement: Retrieve author knowledge and relevant external sources before constructing content; identify
gaps.

DS-04.02 | BR-04.02
Separate semantic content, epistemic assessment, justification and provenance; use supersedes links for
semantic revisions.
Verification: Refuting a proposition preserves its identity and history; a changed proposition links to its
predecessor.
Requirement: Preserve proposition identity across epistemic state changes and track content revisions.

DS-05 Intellectual construction and reasoning
DS-05.01 | BR-05.01
Act records reference intent, input knowledge, outcome and constituent acts; enforce acyclic containment
while allowing shared references.
Verification: A chapter can be inspected as acts and dependencies without reading prose.
Requirement: Construct intellectual acts before generating prose; support atomic and composite acts.

DS-05.02 | BR-05.02
Use Argument as candidate composite act; premise and conclusion are contextual proposition roles;
inference and qualification are typed acts or edges.
Verification: A conclusion can be traced to premises, inference, assumptions, evidence and objections.
Requirement: Represent argumentation and intellectual dependencies.



DS-06 Composition and literary structure
DS-06.01 | BR-06.01
Composition placements reference acts and expressions with contextual order; maintain separate intellectual
dependency edges.
Verification: Reordering paragraphs does not silently alter the underlying argument.
Requirement: Maintain intellectual and literary structures separately with many-to-many mappings.

DS-06.02 | BR-06.02
Version work snapshots and placement edges; export manuscript from selected expression revisions.
Verification: Two works may reference an act without duplicating its identity.
Requirement: Support reusable acts and multiple manuscript versions.

DS-07 Authorial voice and exemplars
DS-07.01 | BR-07.01
Rule records contain symbolic constraint, natural-language realization, applicability, exceptions, conflicts and
justification.
Verification: Prompt construction can resolve applicable rules across all four layers.
Requirement: Represent linguistic, rhetorical, epistemic and conceptual voice rules.

DS-07.02 | BR-07.02
Reference source spans, acts, author decisions and exemplar role; distinguish authentic material from
generated examples.
Verification: A voice rule can be traced to an exact source passage or author correction.
Requirement: Use authentic positive, negative and corrective exemplars.



DS-08 Expression and prompt construction
DS-08.01 | BR-08.01
Expression revisions reference realized acts, selected voice rules, exemplars, text and generation execution.
Verification: Alternative expressions can be generated without changing the proposition or act.
Requirement: Generate prose as a realization of encoded intellectual structure, not as the authoritative
knowledge source.

DS-08.02 | BR-08.02
Assemble intent, knowledge, composition, voice, exemplars and criteria; persist prompt snapshot and
execution references under platform policy.
Verification: A generated passage can be traced to the prompt, model and input versions.
Requirement: Construct prompts from resolved symbolic inputs and preserve execution provenance.

DS-09 Checking, validation and closure
DS-09.01 | BR-09.01
Adapt existing loop interfaces; distinguish termination, justified closure and authority to close; no parallel
lifecycle engine.
Verification: Each operation has input evidence, output, checking, validation and closure evidence or an
explicit unresolved state.
Requirement: Use the canonical loop for research, composition, expression, editing and learning.

DS-09.02 | BR-09.02
Instrument adapters return versioned observations; assess against explicit criteria, record uncertainty and
escalate disputed findings.
Verification: A detector flag alone cannot establish authorship or justify automatic rejection.
Requirement: Separate criteria, instruments and findings; use Pangram as a signal, not proof.



DS-10 Editing and deficiency resolution
DS-10.01 | BR-10.01
Finding references affected object, criterion and observation; confirmed deficiency records adjudication and
resolution loop.
Verification: A missing premise and a stylistic defect route to different appropriate operations.
Requirement: Represent deficiencies as traceable discrepancies and distinguish findings from confirmed
deficiencies.

DS-10.02 | BR-10.02
Expression checks emit findings; route by deficiency type to research, composition, voice or expression loop;
reassess affected closure.
Verification: A discovered unsupported claim triggers research rather than cosmetic rewriting.
Requirement: Allow writing to reveal research and reasoning gaps.

DS-11 Authorial learning
DS-11.01 | BR-11.01
Classify correction as local preference, rule enforcement, exception or candidate rule; use learning loop with
evidence and authority.
Verification: A single correction is not promoted to a universal rule without justification.
Requirement: Learn from author corrections without automatically generalizing local preferences.

DS-11.02 | BR-11.02
Route knowledge and voice updates to distinct graph regions; preserve provenance and review rule changes.
Verification: A new subject fact does not silently modify a voice rule.
Requirement: Separate subject learning from authorial learning.



DS-12 Change propagation and integrity
DS-12.01 | BR-12.01
Record versioned dependency edges and closure witnesses; compute impact, evaluate relevance and reopen
only invalidated or uncertain closures.
Verification: A refuted premise reopens dependent reasoning where justification fails; punctuation alone
does not reopen research.
Requirement: Track dependencies and reassess affected closures when relevant inputs change.

DS-12.02 | BR-12.02
Use immutable revisions or equivalent audit history; mark stale dependencies and retain prior closure
records.
Verification: Previous manuscript and evidence versions remain reconstructable.
Requirement: Preserve history and prevent silent overwrites.

DS-13 Platform integration and traceability
DS-13.01 | BR-13.01
Maintain typed satisfies, realizes and verifies edges; resolve reverse links; bind code references to repository
revision and symbol or artifact identity.
Verification: Every approved requirement resolves to design; implemented design resolves to code and
evidence, with missing links visible.
Requirement: Bind requirements, design, implementation and verification evidence bidirectionally through
persistent identities.

DS-13.02 | BR-13.02
Render versioned graph projections; use existing catalog and change authority; refuse invalid construction
where enforceable and report procedural checks separately.
Verification: A changed requirement marks affected design and code links for reassessment; regenerated
documents show the new baseline.
Requirement: Generate requirements and design documents from the authoritative graph and govern
changes through the same canonical architecture.

Open decisions and integration gates
   Reconcile candidate classes and operations against the current platform catalog and canonical loop
    interfaces; establish actual enforcement points.
   Confirm authority matrix for authorial endorsement, rule generalization, criterion changes, ontology
    extensions and closure.
   Finalize intellectual-act taxonomy, epistemic states, composition cardinalities and revision semantics.
   Select model and Pangram integration, source permissions, retention, security, cost and latency targets.
   Define code registration granularity, deployment checks, telemetry coverage and independent
    verification protocol.
   Establish evaluation corpus of authentic writing and author-reviewed outputs; calibrate fidelity and
    intent-fulfilment criteria.



End-to-end acceptance scenario
Request a chapter distinguishing checking from validation. Resolve author intent and source knowledge;
construct and inspect the distinction and argument; select voice rules and exemplars; generate alternative
prose; check fidelity and validate intent; record closure evidence and authority. Change a premise and verify
selective reopening. Change a requirement and verify impact through design, code and evidence. Missing
links must remain visible, not be treated as successful verification.
