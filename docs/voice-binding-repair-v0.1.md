# Reviewed voice binding repair

The worked-example trial exposed a missing part of the construction loop:
review could reject an exemplar application or criterion, but the implementation
could not return that finding to voice resolution. This bounded operation repairs
that gap under the existing `Faculty` construction owner and `Execution`
coordinator.

`repair-voice` requires delegated operational authorization, the resolved profile,
an explicit committed `max_voice_repairs` budget, a current unclosed construction,
passing conceptual and epistemic review, and a definite rhetorical-plan failure.
Uncertain findings and substantive construction failures cannot enter this route.

The provider receives the fixed acts and claims, full available bound voice
catalog, approved written example at its recorded scope, and the exact failed
review. It returns each existing act ID in order with revised bindings, or a
broader reopening request. The operation cannot change claims, intent, act
purposes or their order. It validates rule/exemplar grounding, preserves unchanged
act and claim identities, and records prior plan, review, input, response, actor,
result and lineage. Stale input or an invalid response remains unresolved.

A successful repair produces an unclosed proposed plan. Fresh construction
review runs before any closure or expression. A passing model review still does
not constitute final author approval.

This implements the existing resolve/check/repair edges and DS-07.01, DS-08.02,
DS-09.01 and DS-10.02. It does not introduce a new scheduler or general intellectual
repair, promote a candidate rule, change the ledger or optimize against a detector.
