# Rules and exemplars govern intellectual construction

The author's clarification requires conceptual and epistemic rules and their
actual exemplars to shape construction, just as rhetorical and linguistic rules
and exemplars shape expression. This implements the existing division in
BR/DS-05, BR/DS-07, BR/DS-08 and the construction/expression stages of the
recorded procedure. It does not introduce an authorial rule or change a ledger.

## Observed difference

The inspected live construction request already contained the full bound rule
and exemplar catalog, plus the approved written reference. Its returned plan
included conceptual and epistemic bindings. The evidence does not support a
claim that those inputs were absent or ignored.

The system instruction nevertheless began: "Construct the intellectual content
before prose, then resolve voice evidence for each act." It did not explicitly
require the conceptual and epistemic examples to guide formation of the
construction. Applications could describe future prose without identifying
the decision they shaped in the encoded argument.

## Correction

The prompt now explicitly instructs construction to use conceptual rule/example
pairs to form concepts, distinctions and relationships, and epistemic pairs to form evidential
commitments, distinguish inference and hypothesis, and place justified
qualifications. Subject evidence and author intent remain the authorities for
content. The examples model how to perform the intellectual work.

The existing `voice_bindings.application` records the demonstrated operation and
the specific construction decision it shapes. The existing criterion identifies
how that decision can be checked in claims or acts. Rhetorical and linguistic
bindings record the planned realization for the expression stage.

Construction review explicitly compares these decisions with the cited
examples. A rule name or an instruction about future sentence style does not
alone demonstrate conceptual or epistemic application. The bounded binding
repair cannot invent a missing intellectual decision; it must request
construction reopening when the required change concerns content.

The provider catalog is projected once, preserving exact exemplars and their
scope. A reported preference, a demonstrated local feature and a complete enacted operation retain their different evidential
roles. Selection does not increase a rule's authority or generalize passage-only
author approval.

## Historical measurements in prompt metadata

The inspected writer requests also contained a historical detector outcome in a
ledger `Review note`, repeated through selected worked-example bindings. The
new scan result had not been sent back to the writer. Therefore a broad claim
that no detector feedback of any kind entered those requests was incorrect.

Provider projections now omit the audit-only rule review note and measurement
fields in historical experiment grounding records. They preserve rule status,
applicability, authority, exact exemplars and provenance. Historical author
judgments remain limited to their passages. The original ledger, graph,
snapshots, prior requests and results remain unchanged. Authentic text is not
edited by a detector-keyword filter.

## Verification and limits

The correction retains the existing owners and schemas. It does not implement
the full epistemic graph, general intellectual repair or flexible sentence
boundaries. It changes no detector threshold or author-approval gate.

Recorded tests and exact-request inspection establish projection and prompt
behavior. They do not establish authorial quality or explain a detector score.
Historical successful passages, author judgments and detector observations
remain the comparison evidence; a new live execution must be recorded
separately and assessed on its actual output.
